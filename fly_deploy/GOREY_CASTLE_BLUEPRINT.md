# 🏰 PROJECT: GOREY CASTLE
## Tactical Web3 Shooter Blueprint

### 🎯 Vision
A high-fidelity, tactical, first-person shooter experience set in the authentic environment of Gorey Castle. The game blends "Call of Duty" style intensity with Web3 ownership and sovereign agent operations.

---

### 💰 The Economic Engine: $PNY
**Primary Token:** `$PNY`
**Role:** The signature token of the Gorey Castle ecosystem.
- **Utility:** Used for gear upgrades, mercenary hiring, and territory bidding.
- **Incentive:** Distributed to teams that successfully capture and hold tactical zones.
- **Sovereignty:** $PNY acts as the proof of influence within the castle's digital territory.

---

### 🗺️ Tactical Map Layout (The Blueprint)

#### Zone A: The Coastal Perimeter (The "Landing")
- **Environment:** Sandy seabed, low tide harbor, stone sea walls.
- **Gameplay:** Wide open, high-risk.
- **Tactical Feature:** **The Sniper's Ridge**. High-ground visibility across the harbor.
- **Objective:** Establish a beachhead.

#### Zone B: The Outer Walls & Walkways (The "Slog")
- **Environment:** Narrow stone corridors, winding ramparts, heavy limestone walls.
- **Gameplay:** Claustrophobic, flank-heavy.
- **Tactical Feature:** **The Breach Point**. A single narrow entry that forces high-intensity CQB.
- **Objective:** Secure the gateway to the keep.

#### Zone C: The Interior Keep (The "Endgame")
- **Environment:** Great halls, spiraling staircases, dark stone vaults.
- **Gameplay:** Verticality, ambush-heavy.
- **Tactical Feature:** **The Murder Holes**. Defensive slots allowing defenders to suppress attackers from above.
- **Objective:** Capture the Sovereignty Core (Final Objective).
