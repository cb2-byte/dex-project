
## Penny the Cat (Bengal F4 SBT)
- **Lineage:** Penny is an F4 SBT (Stud Book Tradition) Bengal. 
- **Legal Status (EU):** F4 Bengals are often classified as "hybrids" or "wild cats" in many EU countries, requiring CITES permits or risking quarantine. Domestic status typically starts at F5.
- **Travel:** Italy is identified as a likely safe destination for F4 Bengals. Iceland and Switzerland are considered high-risk due to strict import laws and the F4 hybrid status.
- **Behavior & Traits:** 
    - Plays "hide and seek" (tags the human when found).
    - Performs "cat kisses" (slow winks), indicating trust and affection.
    - High prey drive: Once caught and brought back a whole pheasant.
    - Appearance: Stunning rosettes, leopard-like markings.
