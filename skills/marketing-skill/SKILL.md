## Skill Steps (Ordered Actions)

### Step 1: Analyze the Request
- **Input**: What's being marketed, to whom, on which channel
- **Output**: Clarified request with product, audience, channel defined
- **Completion Criterion**: User confirms the marketing focus and target

### Step 2: Generate Marketing Assets
- **Copy Types**: Headlines, body text, CTAs, hashtags, emojis
- **Platform-Specific**: Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, Twitter ads, email sequences
- **Output Format**: Structured text with platform markers

### Step 2a: Headline Generation
- Create 3-5 attention-grabbing headlines
- Each under 60 characters
- Include power words and value proposition

### Step 2b: Body Copy
- 2-3 paragraph marketing text
- Problem-solution-benefit framework
- Include social proof where applicable

### Step 2c: Call-to-Action
- 2-3 CTA options
- Urgency and scarcity elements where appropriate

### Step 3: Platform Optimization
- **Twitter**: Thread format, hashtag strategy, emoji usage
- **LinkedIn**: Professional tone, industry keywords, tag suggestions
- **Twitter Ads**: Character limits, visual recommendations
- **Email**: Subject lines, preheader text, segmentation hints

### Step 4: Review and Refine
- User reviews generated copy
- Tone adjustment (professional, playful, urgent, etc.)
- Platform-specific polish
- Final approval criteria met
